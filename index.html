About Me

I'm a Technology Consultant at LKS Next and a PhD in Computer Science from the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU). My research focused on Software Product Lines (SPL) and feature-oriented visualisation, bridging academic rigour with real-world software engineering challenges.

Today I specialise in Generative AI, LLMs, and Data Spaces, helping organisations design and deploy intelligent solutions that are practical, scalable, and aligned with emerging European data-governance frameworks such as Gaia-X and IDSA.

I enjoy the full arc from research to production: exploring novel ideas, building prototypes, and shipping software that makes a tangible difference. When I'm not coding or consulting, I like to write about AI trends and share open-source tools with the community.

Works

Open-source tools developed during my research on Software Product Lines.

SPL · Visualization

CustomDiff

A browser extension that visualises custom differences in annotative Software Product Lines. It highlights feature-specific code regions to help developers understand and compare product variants directly within their workflow, reducing the cognitive overhead of SPL analysis.

View on GitHub
SPL · Visualization

FeatureCloud

A cloud-based platform for sharing and reusing features across Software Product Lines. FeatureCloud enables teams to discover, publish, and integrate SPL features through a centralised repository, fostering collaborative development and reuse of variability assets.
